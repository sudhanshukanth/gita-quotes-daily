Privacy Policy

Bhagavad Gita Quotes Daily (Android app) · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Bhagavad Gita Quotes Daily ("the app") is a free app for reading the Bhagavad Gita. We built it to need as little of your information as possible. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run any servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your chosen language, text size and reminder time, and the first name you may choose to give for the morning greeting
  • Verses you like, personal notes you write, and which verses you have read

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data from your phone.

Daily reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not use a push-notification server and no information is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Widget and saved pictures

The optional home-screen widget shows the day's verse. It is drawn on your phone from the app's own text and sends nothing anywhere.

When you save a verse as a picture or set it as your wallpaper, the app asks Android for permission to add the picture to your photos. It never reads or uploads your existing photos.

Advertising

The app shows small banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad interactions. If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA or UK), you will be asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time. Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Contributions ("Support Us")

Optional contributions are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google shares with us only what is needed to confirm a purchase (such as an order ID). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may send us an anonymous crash report (Android vitals) if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports contain technical details about the crash and your device model, not your personal information.
